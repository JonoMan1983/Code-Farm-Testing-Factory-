Indiemode

A full-site redesign for an independent South African fashion label — and the first build in this portfolio made end-to-end with Claude as a deliberate creative and technical partner.

CLIENTIndiemode
SURFACEResponsive website

The surface problem

The brief: elevate the brand, modernise the layout, and ship fast — without the result looking like every other AI-generated site.

Excavation log

01Structure & copy

Page architecture, navigation hierarchy and UX copy generated through structured Claude prompts, iterated until the tone matched the brand.

Editorial dark mode, bold serif type and a neon palette — set by me before a line of code.

HTML, CSS and interaction logic authored by Claude, with me directing layout, hierarchy and brand fidelity at every step.

Structure, visual design and live deployment — a typical week of build compressed into two days.
